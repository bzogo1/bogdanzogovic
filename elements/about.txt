animated-blob

HTML: Full-screen <canvas>.
CSS: Black background, hidden overflow, monospace styling.
JS: Draws an animated, irregular neon blob using trigonometric curves. Its hue, complexity, and speed are randomized on load and whenever the canvas is clicked.
Appearance: A glowing, colorful organic outline slowly morphing against a black background.
background

HTML: Full-screen canvas inside #fixed-bg.
CSS: Centers and scales the canvas to cover the viewport; black fallback background.
JS: Creates a grid of image fragments from a selected image. The fragments enlarge or shrink around the pointer, creating a magnifying/ripple-like distortion. It also draws optional dots and includes a Tweakpane control panel.
Appearance: A full-screen photographic background made of square tiles that react to cursor movement.
Instructions: A note requesting this background be applied across the website except for the hero/top section.
coffee-jar

HTML: Inline SVG illustration of a coffee pot with a face, arms, legs, reflections, and coffee inside.
CSS: Centers the SVG on a pale cream background.
Appearance: A large kawaii-style smiling coffee jar/pot with simple black outlines and a warm cream/brown palette.
cool-animation

HTML: SVG containing a grid of masked circles.
CSS: Centers the SVG at half the viewport size.
JS: Uses GSAP to clone the circles into columns and animate their vertical positions and scale. Clicking pauses or resumes the animation and changes the fill color.
Appearance: A tiled, wave-like field of circles that expands and contracts in staggered motion.
fluid-text

HTML: Text input, density/speed/turbulence/font-size sliders, and a pause button.
CSS: Chartreuse background, custom cursor, animated grain texture, and bottom control bar.
JS: Uses Three.js and a fragment shader to render animated fluid contour lines inside user-provided text. Pointer movement creates a local swell; clicks create ripples; sliders alter the shader.
Appearance: Large neon-green typography filled with animated lavender liquid-like contour patterns.
gallery

HTML: A 14-item photo gallery using images from picsum.photos.
CSS: Arranges photos as clipped hexagons with responsive wrapping.
Appearance and behavior: A dark editorial gallery of grayscale hexagonal images. Hovering or focusing a tile lifts it, enlarges and brightens the image, removes the dark overlay, and reveals a title and category caption.
highliting-effect

HTML: The phrase “make something different.”
CSS: Uses Poppins and Playwrite IS fonts. The word “different” is styled as handwritten text with a large green circular highlight that animates through a conic mask.
Appearance: Minimal light-gray typography with a playful handwritten word surrounded by an animated green ring.
Note: The folder name is misspelled as highliting-effect.
horizontal-scroll-images

HTML: A “Let’s connect” section with seven product images, a horizontally scrollable carousel, and an email form.
CSS: Claymorphism styling with raised/inset shadows, rounded product panels, responsive layouts, and light/dark theme variables.
JS: Uses GSAP and ScrollTrigger for reveal animations. The carousel applies 3D rotation, scaling, depth, and opacity based on each item’s distance from center. It also supports pointer dragging.
Appearance: A soft, neumorphic product carousel that behaves like a 3D coverflow.
image-animation

HTML: A scrollable page containing a poolside image, heading, and descriptive text.
CSS: Initially clips the image into a centered rounded rectangle and hides the text below its final position.
JS: Uses GSAP ScrollTrigger to pin the section while scrolling. The image expands to fill the screen, then the heading and paragraph fade and slide into view.
Appearance: A cinematic scroll transition from a small rounded image window into a full-screen photograph with overlaid copy.
image-dots-effect

HTML: Full-screen canvas.
CSS: Black full-screen background with a centered canvas.
JS: Loads an image, divides it into a grid of square regions, and redraws those regions around the pointer. It can optionally fade tiles and draw dots. Tweakpane controls image choice, tile size, fading, and dot color.
Appearance: An image composed of square tiles that deform around the cursor, with optional dot patterns.
wave-background

HTML: Four layered wave elements, each containing spray particles.
CSS: Builds a teal-to-sand gradient “beach” background using animated CSS shapes, clipping paths, custom properties, and keyframes.
Appearance: A full-screen abstract seascape with several translucent teal waves moving horizontally and changing shape. White spray-like particles animate along the wave edges in browsers supporting the required CSS features.
The small bottom-right author badge is separate from the actual experiment.
Several demos depend on external libraries or assets: GSAP, ScrollTrigger, Three.js, Tweakpane, Google Fonts, Unsplash, Shopify-hosted images, and Picsum photos.
